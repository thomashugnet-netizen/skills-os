Career areas

Six kinds of work. Here is what each one involves.

Store teams

Register, coffee bar, stocking and keeping the store spotless. Busy mornings, steady afternoons, a lot of regulars who learn your name.

  • Shifts: early, mid or late, set two weeks ahead
  • Pay: from $15.00/hour
  • Experience needed: none, we train on paid time
What a shift looks like

Food service

Made-to-order breakfast and lunch from a short menu. Food safety and speed matter more than fancy technique.

  • Shifts: early starts, done by mid-afternoon
  • Pay: from $15.50/hour
  • Food handler card: we help you get it
See food service jobs

Store leadership

Shift leads, assistant managers and store managers. You run the floor, the schedule and the people who make the store work.

  • Most leaders started as team members
  • Pay: from $18.00/hour for shift leads
  • Bonus tied to store results
See leadership jobs

Distribution and driving

Warehouse selectors and CDL drivers who supply every store. Physical work, predictable routes, home most nights.

  • Warehouse: fixed shifts, overtime available
  • Drivers: Class A CDL required
  • Pay: shown on every listing
See distribution jobs

Maintenance and trades

Technicians who keep coolers, fuel equipment and kitchens running across a region. You plan your own route each day.

  • Company vehicle and tools
  • On-call rotation shared across the team
  • Certifications paid for
See trades jobs

Office and corporate

Merchandising, finance, people and technology teams who support the stores. Many started on a store floor.

  • Hybrid schedules for most roles
  • Store visits are part of the job
  • Salaried, pay range on every listing
See corporate jobs

Career growth

How people move up in stores

Typical timings, not promises. Each step comes with paid training.

  1. Team member
    Learn the store, the register and the coffee bar.
  2. Shift lead
    Run a shift and open or close the store. Often after 6 to 12 months.
  3. Assistant manager
    Ordering, scheduling and training new hires.
  4. Store manager
    Own the results of one store and its team.
  5. District leader
    Support several stores and their managers.

How we hire

From applying to your first shift

  1. 5 minutes

    Apply from your phone
