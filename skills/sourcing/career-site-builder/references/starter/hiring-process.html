How we hire

Four steps, usually about a week from applying to your first shift.

  1. 5 minutes

    Apply

    Pick a job, answer a few questions about availability and travel, and send it. No resume needed for store and food jobs.

  2. Within 48 hours

    Hear back

    We text you with a link to choose an interview time. If the job is filled, we tell you and suggest others nearby.

  3. 20 minutes

    Meet the manager

    A conversation at the store or distribution center about the job, your schedule and your questions.

  4. About one week

    Start

    Complete your paperwork online, then work your first shift alongside a trainer. Training is paid.

Accommodations

If you need an adjustment to apply or interview, such as more time, a different format or help with the online form, contact careers@example.com. Asking does not affect how your application is considered.

Applying on our hiring partner's site

When you click Apply, you finish your application on our secure applicant tracking site. It looks different from this one; that is expected.
