Questions applicants ask us

Straight answers. If yours is not here, ask the store manager at your interview.

Do I need experience to work in a store?

No. Store team and food service jobs include paid training. Leadership, driving and trades jobs list their requirements on each listing.

How far ahead will I know my schedule?

Schedules are posted 14 days ahead. You tell us your availability when you apply, and you can update it later.

How much does the job pay?

Every job listing shows the hourly rate or salary range for that role and location.

How old do I have to be?

At least 16 for store team jobs and 18 for roles that sell age-restricted products or drive.

How long does it take to get hired?

Most people hear back within 48 hours and start within about a week of applying.

How do I check my application?

Use the link in your confirmation text or email, or go to application status.

I need help applying or an accommodation. Who do I ask?

See accommodations. We will make reasonable adjustments at any step.
