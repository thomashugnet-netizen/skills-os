A typical shift

Mornings are the rush: coffee, breakfast and fuel customers on their way to work. Afternoons are for restocking, cleaning and getting ready for the evening. You will work the register, but you will rarely do one thing for a whole shift.

Who does well here

People who like being busy, can stay friendly when a line forms and notice when something needs doing. Experience in retail or food helps; it is not required.

The facts

  • Pay: from $15.00/hour, shown on every listing
  • Shifts: early (5am), mid or late, posted two weeks ahead
  • Hours: part-time and full-time
  • Physical: on your feet, lifting cases up to 40 lb
  • Training: paid, with a trainer for your first shifts
  • Next step: shift lead, often after 6 to 12 months
