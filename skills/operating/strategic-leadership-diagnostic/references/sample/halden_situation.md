# Situation: Halden Market, 11 November 2026

Halden Market is fictional. This is the account the diagnostic was run on: what the
person brought, then their answers to its questions, kept word for word, because
every finding has to trace back to something said here.

## What I brought

I'm Director of Talent Acquisition and People Operations at Halden Market, 46 grocery
stores across three states. I report to the CHRO and I own recruiting, onboarding and
the HRIS. I don't manage store managers; they report to district managers, who report
to the COO.

Our COO wants recruiting to increase applicant volume because several locations keep
losing new hires. In the October ops review he asked me to "get twice the applicants
into the six worst stores before the holiday peak". I believe we need to examine the
employee experience, but I haven't built a convincing case or aligned with Operations.
I'm sure the real problem is the stores, not the job ads.

The next leadership meeting is on 24 November. The COO and the CHRO will decide whether
to fund an extra $60,000 of job advertising for Q1.

## Answers to the diagnostic's questions

**What do you know about the six stores, in numbers?**
Applicant volume at the six stores is flat year on year, about 140 applications a
month each. 41% of hires at those six stores leave within 30 days, against 19% across
the other 40 stores. The funnel drop-off analysis we ran in September found the gap
concentrated under three store managers, and it held within role.

**What have you done so far, and how did people respond?**
I sent the COO a two-page memo saying the problem is store leadership, not sourcing.
He didn't reply. One district manager told me HR is blaming the stores for a
recruiting problem. I haven't spoken to the store managers at the six stores.

**What would support from you look like for those store managers?**
I don't know what they need. I plan to propose a manager onboarding program in Q1.

**How is this landing with your own team?**
My recruiters are exhausted, they feel they are being asked to fill a leaking bucket.
I haven't said anything to them about what happens next.

**Have you done anything like this before that worked?**
Last year we piloted a first-week buddy program in four stores, and 30-day turnover
there fell from 33% to 21% over two quarters. We never rolled it out.

**Where does the CHRO stand?**
We haven't discussed it. She saw the memo.

**What would change your mind?**
If the six stores also have a longer wait from offer to first shift, it could be a
scheduling problem and not a manager problem. I haven't checked that.
